About Us

Preserving tradition, craftsmanship and history through beautiful metal artifacts.

Heritage Metal Artifacts is a dedicated antique and traditional metal artifacts shop based in Bengaluru. We offer a carefully selected collection of traditional metalware, decorative artifacts and heritage pieces.

Our collection celebrates Indian craftsmanship and traditional artistry. Every artifact is selected with attention to its design, workmanship and cultural value.
